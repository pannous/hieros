DH / DHa — Darius I, gold & silver foundation tablets, Hamadan (Ecbatana)
==========================================================================
Museum: National Museum of Iran, Tehran. Old siglum DH (Kent 1953);
current siglum DHa (Schmitt 2009, "DHa ex-DH"). Two duplicate deposits,
gold (DHaG) and silver (DHas), text identical to DPh (Persepolis
Apadana foundation tablets) apart from Old Persian line-division
(DH/DHa = 8 OP lines, DPh = 10 OP lines; Elamite 7 lines and Akkadian
8 lines are identical in both). Physical tablets were cut into pieces
in antiquity/modern looting (gold: ~20 fragments, silver: 8, 7 survive)
and reassembled before study (Unvala, Jame Jamshed, 30 Sept 1926).

Sources
-------
- Kent, R.G., Old Persian: Grammar, Texts, Lexicon (1953), pp.111b-112a, 148.
- Schmitt, R., Die altpersischen Inschriften der Achaimeniden (2009), pp.11, 98-99.
- Vallat, F., Corpus des inscriptions royales en elamite achemenide (1977), pp.24, 146-148.
  (Vallat's edition is of DPh; text stated identical to DH by Vallat/Schmitt/de.wikipedia.)
- Schmidt, E.F., Persepolis I (OIP 68, 1953), pp.69-70 (English paraphrase only,
  notes Babylonian variant "with the (other) gods").
- CII (Corpus Inscriptionum Iranicarum) publication list, SOAS 2025: confirms
  vol. II (Babylonian) and vol. III-IV (Elamite/Egyptian) fascicles for the
  Persepolis/Hamadan foundation texts were NEVER published.
Akkadian/Babylonian transliteration: NOT FOUND in any accessible published
source (see above) — not included below. Do not treat its absence here as
an oversight; it appears not to exist in citable form outside possibly
Sidney Smith, JRAS 1926, 433-6, and Weissbach, ZfA 37 (1927), 291-4
(pre-JSTOR, not retrieved).

  pè (accented, u-is-pè-na and sa-ak-ka-pè) -> fed as "ni" -> 𒉌 (NI,
        the standard sign for the bé/pè4 homophone 𒁉?? — distinct from the
        unaccented "pe" in ak-ka-pe, which keeps the tool's PE sign 𒄫)

Remaining open item: "tù" (in mi-ut-tù, line 4) has no confirmed
distinct Unicode sign found in either pass — plain TU (𒌅, used above)
is a placeholder, not a verified reading of this specific homophone
(often indexed TU4 in Borger's MesZL). Needs a direct MesZL/CDLI
sign-list lookup before treating as settled.

Sign choices beyond the above are only as reliable as abc/cuneiform.list
and the Unicode sign-name table; not checked against Vallat's
photographic plates sign-by-sign. Treat as a working restoration, not a
certified critical edition.

Translation (from Vallat's French, p.147-148):
1-3. (I am) Darius, the great king, king of kings, king of the lands,
     son of Hystaspes, the Achaemenid.
3-7. Darius the king says: "This is the kingship which I hold, from
     the Scythians who are beyond Sogdiana as far as Ethiopia (Kush),
     from India as far as Sardis, which Ahuramazda gave to me, (he)
     who is the greatest of the gods. May Ahuramazda protect me and
     my palace."


3. BABYLONIAN / AKKADIAN — not published, not included (see Sources above).
Only known datum (Schmidt 1953, p.70 fn.2): Babylonian version inserts
"with the (other) gods" after "the greatest of the gods" — a genuine
textual variant absent from OP and Elamite.
