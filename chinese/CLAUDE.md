In this project folder, we keep track of our progress for learning Chinese and write down general rules of how to communicate in Chinese.

Use Simplified Chinese with Hanyu Pinyin consistently.
Present Chinese vocabulary with its English meaning.
Prefer brief interactive exercises using single words or short phrases for spoken practice.

work with @chinese.mnemonics.tsv these are rated by level of overlap.

mnemonics work especially well in clusters. e.g.
哈 hā haha
合 hé heim, chamber
峇 kē cave
合 gě grain ≈  gram (volume)
给 gěi give <> dá
㾑 chill, cold
谷	gǔ	gorge valley

And ideally mnemonics should have associations in all dimensions, form, phonetics, semantics, ...
Add new mnemonics whenever we discover them.

In progress.md keep track of how well I understand certain words. Don't overlearn words that are understood, and don't do too many new words at once. Make it like a digital Anki card with repetition count per Vocabulary entry.

At the end of each session or in between, write down the progress.md
