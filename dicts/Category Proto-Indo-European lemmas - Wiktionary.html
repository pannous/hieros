Proto-Indo-European lemmas, categorized by their part of speech.

  • Category:Proto-Indo-European adjectives: Proto-Indo-European terms that give attributes to nouns, extending their definitions.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European adverbs: Proto-Indo-European terms that modify clauses, sentences and phrases directly.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European conjunctions: Proto-Indo-European terms that connect words, phrases or clauses together.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European determiners: Proto-Indo-European terms that narrow down, within the conversational context, the referent of following noun.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European interjections: Proto-Indo-European terms that express emotions, sounds, etc. as exclamations.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European morphemes: Proto-Indo-European word-elements used to form full words.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European nouns: Proto-Indo-European terms that indicate people, beings, things, places, phenomena, qualities or ideas.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European numerals: Proto-Indo-European terms that quantify nouns.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European particles: Proto-Indo-European terms that do not belong to any of the inflected grammatical word classes, often lacking their own grammatical functions and forming other parts of speech or expressing the relationship between clauses.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European pronouns: Proto-Indo-European terms that refer to and substitute nouns.
  • Category:Proto-Indo-European verbs: Proto-Indo-European terms that indicate actions, occurrences or states.
